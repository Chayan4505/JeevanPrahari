How It Works:
=============

1. When you click on a report card in the Alerts page
2. The ReportDetailsModal opens
3. Each report automatically looks for its photo: /report-photo-{ID}.jpg
4. If found → displays the photo at the top
5. If not found → shows gradient placeholder with camera emoji (📸)

File Format:
============
- JPG or PNG format recommended
- Square, landscape, or portrait orientation all work
- Recommended minimum size: 600x400px

Example:
If you click on Report #5, it will look for: report-photo-5.jpg

Just upload your 17 photos with the exact filenames above, and they'll automatically appear!
